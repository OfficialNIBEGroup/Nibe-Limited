ABOUT US

Welcome to Bharat Bio Gas

At Bharat Bio Gas, we believe every agricultural residue has the potential to become a valuable source of clean energy. Bharat Bio Gas is developing advanced Compressed Biogas (CBG) infrastructure that converts agricultural biomass into clean, renewable fuel and high-value organic bio-fertilizers. Through cutting-edge technology and sustainable engineering, we are contributing to India's clean energy transition while creating long-term value for farmers, industries and society.

Our integrated CBG project combines advanced anaerobic digestion, modern gas purification systems and intelligent process engineering to convert biomass into high-quality compressed biogas that meets national fuel quality standards. Beyond renewable fuel production, our facilities recover nutrients from organic waste to produce premium organic bio-fertilizers, supporting sustainable farming and reducing environmental impact.

Supporting India's SATAT Initiative

Bharat Bio Gas aligns with the SATAT (Sustainable Alternative Towards Affordable Transportation) initiative launched by the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas (MoPNG) to promote Compressed Bio Gas (CBG) as a clean and sustainable transportation fuel. Through advanced CBG production, we contribute to renewable energy generation, agricultural waste management and India's vision of a greener, self-reliant future.

Driving India's Renewable Energy Transition

Bharat Bio Gas is committed to developing integrated renewable energy infrastructure that converts biomass and municipal waste into clean energy solutions. Our projects are designed to support sustainable development, resource recovery and environmental conservation.

OUR TECHNOLOGIES

From Biomass To CBG

1

Anaerobic Digestion

Converts agricultural biomass into biogas through a natural biological process.

2

Biogas Upgradation

Removes impurities from raw biogas to produce high-quality CBG.

3

Gas Purification

Removes moisture, CO₂ and H₂S to ensure clean and efficient fuel.

4

CBG Compression

Compresses purified CBG for safe storage and easy transportation.

5

Organic Bio-Fertilizer Recovery

Converts the remaining digestate into nutrient-rich organic bio-fertilizer for sustainable farming.

Compressed Biogas (CBG)

Clean. Renewable. Reliable.

Bharat Bio Gas produces upgraded biogas through the anaerobic digestion of agricultural biomass. The gas is purified to achieve a methane content of ≥99% and complies with IS 16087:2025 quality standards, making it suitable for automotive, industrial, and commercial applications.

Key Features
  • Renewable alternative to CNG
  • Methane Purity ≥99%
  • High Calorific Value
  • Low Carbon Emissions
  • IS 16087:2025 Compliant
  • Safe & Reliable Fuel
Applications
  • City Gas Distribution (CGD)
  • Automotive Fuel
  • Industrial Heating
  • Boilers & Furnaces
  • Commercial Fuel
  • Power Generation

Solid Fermented Organic Manure (FOM)

Restoring Soil Naturally

Solid Fermented Organic Manure is produced from nutrient-rich digestate generated during the CBG production process. It improves soil fertility, enhances microbial activity, increases water retention and promotes sustainable agricultural practices.

Benefits
  • Rich Organic Nutrients
  • Improves Soil Structure
  • Enhances Crop Productivity
  • Eco-Friendly
  • Reduces Chemical Fertilizer Dependency
  • Supports Sustainable Farming
Applications
  • Agriculture
  • Horticulture
  • Plantations
  • Organic Farming
  • Landscaping

Liquid Fermented Organic Manure (LFOM)

Natural Nutrition For Better Crop Growth

Liquid Fermented Organic Manure is a nutrient-rich liquid bio-fertilizer recovered during digestate processing. It supplies essential nutrients to crops while improving soil biological activity and promoting healthy plant growth.

Key Benefits
  • Fast Nutrient Absorption
  • Improves Root Development
  • Enhances Crop Yield
  • Supports Soil Microorganisms
  • Suitable for Drip Irrigation
  • Environmentally Sustainable
Applications
  • Field Crops
  • Fruits & Vegetables
  • Greenhouses
  • Drip Irrigation
  • Organic Agriculture

Elemental Sulfur

High-Purity Sulfur Recovery

During the gas purification process, sulfur compounds are safely removed from biogas. The recovered elemental sulfur can be utilized for industrial applications and fertilizer manufacturing, contributing to efficient resource recovery.

Benefits
  • High Purity
  • Resource Recovery
  • Environmentally Responsible
  • Industrial Grade Quality
Applications
  • Fertilizer Industry
  • Chemical Manufacturing
  • Industrial Processing

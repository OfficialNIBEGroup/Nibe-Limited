SPACE SYSTEM

Satellite Based Intelligence

Optical • SAR • RF / SIGINT Intelligence

Optical Intelligence

BlackSky Gen-3 Class

The planned constellation of more than 15 satellites in inclined orbit provides continuous coverage of sovereign areas of interest. Delivering imagery with up to 35 cm resolution, the system enables precise identification of terrain, assets, and infrastructure changes. These capabilities support defence surveillance, border monitoring, urban planning, agriculture, disaster management, and advanced geospatial intelligence.

SAR Intelligence

Capella Acadia Class

A constellation of 6+ Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) satellites provides all-weather, day-and-night imaging capabilities. Combining ultra-high resolution with wide-area coverage, the system enables persistent surveillance of borders, maritime regions, critical infrastructure, and adversary activity. With sub-0.5 metre resolution, it can detect ships, illegal fishing, dark vessels, and oil spills even during heavy cloud cover or nighttime operations.

RF / SIGINT

Cluster Flying

The RF/SIGINT constellation comprises planned clusters of three satellites, covering frequencies from 30 MHz to 18 GHz. Supporting VHF, UHF, L, S, C, X, and Ku bands, the system captures the majority of man-made radio frequency emissions. It is designed for Electronic Intelligence (ELINT), Communications Intelligence (COMINT), and Signals Intelligence (SIGINT) missions across land, naval, and airborne operational environments.

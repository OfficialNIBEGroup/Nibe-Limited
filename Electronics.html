CABLE HARNESS

Cable Harness Project

Precision Engineered Cable Harness Solutions

NIBE Defence & Aerospace Limited manufactures high-quality cable harnesses for Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), supporting mission-critical defence programs. Built to stringent military standards, these cable harnesses ensure reliable electrical connectivity, durability and superior operational performance.

  • 16 types of cable harnesses developed for BEL Machilipatnam's internal production projects.
  • Production of 486 cable harnesses per squadron for AKASH Missile System.
  • Defence-grade manufacturing with strict quality assurance.
  • High reliability for mission-critical applications.
ELECTRONICS DIVISION

INTEGRATED MOBILE SHELTER PROJECT (IMS)

Rugged military computing and networking systems engineered for mobile shelters, command centres and mission-critical defence applications.

Ruggedized Panel PC

The IMS MK-III Ruggedized Panel PC is a powerful computer built for demanding military environments. It delivers reliable processing performance and advanced graphics while supporting multiple rugged input/output interfaces.

  • Intel® 9th Gen Core i7-9850HE Processor
  • 16 GB DDR4 RAM
  • On-board NVIDIA T1000 GPGPU
  • Multiple Rugged I/O Ports

Ruggedized Dual Display Consoles

Designed using SwAP technology for military shelter vehicles, ships and submarines, providing an efficient command & control workstation.

  • Space for 2 Ruggedized Panel PCs
  • Dual Keyboard with Touchpad & Biometric
  • Housing for Rugged Ethernet Switch
  • Integrated Power Distribution Box

Ruggedized Ethernet Switches

High-performance managed Gigabit Ethernet Switch providing secure LAN connectivity for rugged defence applications.

  • 16-Port Managed Gigabit Ethernet Switch
  • 12 × 10/100/1000 Mbps Copper PoE Ports
  • Power Input : AC 230V (180V–264V)
  • 0.3A Power Consumption
MODULAR BRIDGE PROJECT

Modular Bridge Project

Rugged Electronic Control & Distribution Units

NIBE Defence & Aerospace Limited designs and manufactures the four core electronic units of the Modular Bridge Project, delivering reliable power distribution, control and sensing capability for mobile bridging systems.

  • Main Distribution Panel for centralised electrical control.
  • Power Distribution Unit for reliable power routing and protection.
  • Joystick Console and Sensors for precise operator control.
  • Launch Vehicle Junction Box for secure field connectivity.

Cable Harness & Interconnect Systems

Data, Power & RF Harness with Circular Connectors

NIBE Defence & Aerospace Limited manufactures rugged interconnect harnesses built around military-grade circular connectors, supporting data, power and RF connectivity across defence platforms.
